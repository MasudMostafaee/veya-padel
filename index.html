02 / FIRST TIME PLAYING?

Everybody starts somewhere.

Padel is easy to pick up from day one. Here is what you need to know:

Zero gear required

Top-tier rental rackets and match balls are waiting at reception. Just show up in clean, flat athletic sneakers.

Simple, fast rules

Underhand serve below the waist. Glass walls keep the ball in play. Standard tennis scoring (15-30-40). Long rallies in 10 minutes.

Get assessed & start right

Join group coaching or request a private coach. We assess your background to place you with the right peer group.
